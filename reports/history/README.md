# Preserved historical reports

`pre_rujaf_update/final_report.md` and `pre_rujaf_update/data_quality.md` are exact copies of the reports
before the RUJAF business clarification. They intentionally retain the **then-unresolved** entity interpretation;
the current reports one directory above supersede that interpretation. Historical numerical results remain valid
under their documented limitations and have not been edited to reflect a different business story.

Links inside these untouched copies are relative to their original `reports/` location. Original tables, figures,
model artifacts, notebook and raw datasets remain in place; SHA-256 fingerprints are recorded in
`../../artifacts/historical_results_manifest.json`. The original `reports/test_results.xml` is also retained.
